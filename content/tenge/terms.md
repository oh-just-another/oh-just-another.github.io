## Exchange rates

Rates in Tenge are indicative reference values from public sources
([Frankfurter](https://frankfurter.dev), with
[exchange-api](https://github.com/fawazahmed0/exchange-api) as a fallback),
updated daily and cached on your device. They are provided for information only:
banks, exchange offices and card issuers apply their own rates and fees, and the
numbers in the app may be out of date while you are offline. Don’t rely on Tenge
alone for financial decisions.
