This policy covers the website **ohjustanother.site** and everything under it:
the home page, the [Tenge](/tenge/) pages, and the [diagram](/diagram/) docs and
playground. Apps have their own policies — for the Tenge app, see the
[Tenge Privacy Policy](/tenge/privacy/).
{.lead}

## Analytics

The site uses **Google Analytics 4** to count visits and see which pages are read.
When you first open a page, a small card asks whether analytics cookies are allowed.

- **Until you answer, or if you choose “No, thanks”**, Google Analytics runs without
  cookies: Google receives cookieless signals about the page view (the page, time,
  browser and device type, approximate region) with no identifier that links your
  visits together. Google uses them to estimate totals.
- **If you choose “Allow”**, Google Analytics also sets first-party cookies
  (`_ga`, `_ga_*`) so that repeat visits can be told apart from new ones.

Advertising features are off in every case: no ad cookies, no ad personalization,
and the data is not used for advertising. Google processes your IP address to
deliver the service; Google Analytics 4 does not log or store IP addresses.
Analytics data is kept for the retention period set in Google Analytics
(2 months) and then deleted. How Google handles this data is described in
[Google’s Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy) and
[How Google uses information from sites that use its services](https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites).

**Changing your choice:** <a href="#" data-analytics-settings>open the analytics
question again</a>, or clear this site’s data in your browser. The choice is
stored only in your browser (local storage) and applies to all pages of the site.

## Hosting

The site is hosted on GitHub Pages. Like any web host, GitHub receives your IP
address when serving pages and may keep it in its logs for security purposes, as
described in the [GitHub Privacy Statement](https://docs.github.com/site-policy/privacy-policies/github-general-privacy-statement).

## Nothing else

No accounts, no forms, no ads, no other trackers or third-party scripts.
